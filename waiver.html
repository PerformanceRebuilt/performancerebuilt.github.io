01

Nature of Services

Performance Rebuilt Aus provides online fitness coaching services, including personalised training programs, nutrition guidance, and progress tracking. Coaches are fitness professionals. Nothing provided constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

02

Pre-Exercise Health Screening (PAR-Q)

Before being granted access to any training program, you are required to complete the PAR-Q provided during onboarding. Program access will not be activated until it has been submitted.

By completing the PAR-Q, you confirm that:

  • You have answered all questions truthfully and to the best of your knowledge
  • If the PAR-Q identifies health risk factors, you will obtain written medical clearance before commencing any program
  • You will notify your coach immediately if your health status changes after submitting the PAR-Q
  • You understand that providing false or incomplete information may result in your access being suspended

If you have a diagnosed medical condition, Performance Rebuilt Aus can only provide services if you present a medical clearance stating your conditions are stable and you are fit to participate.

03

Acknowledgment of Risks

You acknowledge that participation in physical exercise and changes to dietary intake carry inherent risks, including:

  • Muscle strains, sprains, and other soft tissue injuries
  • Joint injuries including to the knee, shoulder, back, and hip
  • Cardiovascular events including elevated heart rate and in rare cases, cardiac events
  • Dizziness, nausea, or fainting during or after exercise
  • Aggravation of pre-existing medical conditions
  • Adverse reactions to changes in nutrition or caloric intake
  • Psychological impacts related to body image or performance pressure
04

Medical Clearance

  • You have obtained, or will obtain, written medical clearance if you have any pre-existing conditions, injuries, or health concerns
  • You are not aware of any physical condition preventing you from participating in moderate to vigorous activity
  • You will immediately cease any exercise causing pain or unusual symptoms and seek medical advice
  • You will inform your coach of any changes in health status
