Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 1 July 2026

Section 01

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

Performance Rebuilt Aus ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the Performance Rebuilt Aus platform.

Health and fitness data is classified as sensitive information under Australian law. We take additional care in handling this data and will only collect, use, or disclose it with your consent or as otherwise permitted by law.

Section 02

What Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Name and email address (for account creation)
  • Body weight, body measurements, and progress photos
  • Nutrition data including calorie and macronutrient intake
  • Workout and exercise logs
  • Health goals and fitness history
  • Pre-exercise health screening responses (PAR-Q)
  • Messages and communications sent through the Platform

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device type and browser information
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Usage data including features accessed and time spent on the Platform
  • Acceptance records including timestamp, IP address, and document version accepted at account activation
Section 03

How We Use Your Information

We collect and use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Deliver personalised coaching, training programs, and nutrition guidance
  • Track your fitness progress over time
  • Communicate with you about your program and account
  • Maintain records of your health screening and consent for legal and safety purposes
  • Improve the Platform and our services
  • Comply with legal obligations

We will not use your sensitive health information for any purpose other than delivering the service to you, without your explicit consent.

Section 04

How We Store Your Information

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Row-level security controls on all database tables
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit
  • Access controls limiting data visibility to you and your assigned coach only

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active, or as required by law. Upon account deletion, your data will be removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

Section 05

Overseas Disclosure and Third-Party Processors

We use the following third-party service providers to operate the Platform:

  • Supabase (cloud database) — servers located in Australia (ap-southeast-2 region). Your data is stored in Australia.
  • Vercel (web hosting and deployment) — Vercel's infrastructure may route requests through servers located outside Australia, including in the United States or Europe. By using the Platform, you consent to this transfer.

Where personal information is disclosed to overseas recipients, Performance Rebuilt Aus remains accountable for ensuring those recipients handle your information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles, in accordance with APP 8. We require all third-party processors to maintain confidentiality obligations and appropriate data security standards.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

Section 06

Progress Photos

Progress photos you upload are stored securely and are only accessible to you and your assigned coach. We will never share, publish, or use your progress photos for any marketing or commercial purpose without your explicit written consent.

Section 07

Your Privacy Rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal obligations)
  • Opt out of any direct marketing communications
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at performancerebuilt@gmail.com.

Section 08

Notifiable Data Breaches

We are subject to the Notifiable Data Breaches (NDB) scheme under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in serious harm to any affected individuals, we will:

  • Contain and assess the breach as quickly as practicable
  • Notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as soon as practicable
  • Notify all affected individuals directly, providing details of the breach and recommended steps they can take to protect themselves

We maintain internal procedures for identifying, assessing, and responding to data breaches. If you believe your information may have been compromised, please contact us immediately at performancerebuilt@gmail.com.

Section 09

Cookies and Tracking

The Platform uses session cookies and local storage to maintain your login session and preferences. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell your browsing data. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect Platform functionality.

Section 10

Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Section 11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes via email or through the Platform. Where changes are material, existing users will be required to re-accept the updated policy before continuing to use the Platform.
