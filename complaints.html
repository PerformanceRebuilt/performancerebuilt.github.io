We genuinely care about the experience every client has with Performance Rebuilt. When something doesn't land the way it should — whether it's the coaching, the communication, or just a feeling that you weren't supported — that matters to us.

This page exists because we believe clients deserve a clear, fair way to raise concerns. And we believe that if we've fallen short, it's on us to listen, take it seriously, and do something about it. Not bury it in process.

The Process

How it works.

If something's gone wrong, here's what happens from the moment you get in touch.

01
Reach out

Email us at admin@performancerebuilt.com.au or use the contact form on this site. Tell us what happened, when, and how it made you feel. The more context, the better we can help.

02
We acknowledge

You'll hear back from us within 7 business days confirming we've received your complaint and that we're looking into it. No automated replies — a real response from a real person.

03
We review it fairly

We take the time to understand what happened from your side. We may reach out with a question or two. We won't dismiss anything without taking a proper look.

04
We work toward resolution

Our goal is a fair outcome, communicated clearly. We'll let you know where things are at throughout — and if we've got it wrong, we'll say so.
