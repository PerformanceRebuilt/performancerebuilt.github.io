Complaints Procedure

Effective Date: 1 July 2026

Our Commitment

We take complaints seriously.

Performance Rebuilt Aus is committed to providing a high standard of service. If something hasn't met your expectations, we want to hear about it and work to put it right.

The Process

Step by step.

01
Submit your complaint

Contact us by email at admin@performancerebuilt.com.au or through the contact form on this website. Please include your name, a description of the issue, and any relevant dates.

02
Acknowledgement

We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within 7 business days of receiving it.

03
Investigation

We will review the circumstances of your complaint thoroughly and fairly, and may contact you for additional information.

04
Resolution

We will work to resolve your complaint promptly and keep you informed throughout. Our goal is a fair outcome as quickly as possible.

Australian Consumer Law

Your rights are protected.

Nothing in this complaints procedure limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to remedies including repair, replacement, or refund where a service fails to meet consumer guarantees.
