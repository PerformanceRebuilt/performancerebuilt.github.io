PAR-Q Health Screening

This form must be completed before commencing any training program with Performance Rebuilt Aus. Your answers help ensure the program is safe and appropriate for you specifically.

Confidential. The information you provide is stored securely, accessible only to you and your assigned coach, and used solely for designing a safe and appropriate training program. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Personal details

Health screening questions

Please answer YES or NO to each question honestly. If you answer YES to any question, please provide details in the field that appears.

Q1. Has a doctor ever said that you have a heart condition and that you should only perform physical activity recommended by a doctor?

Q2. Do you feel pain in your chest when you perform physical activity?

Q3. In the past month, have you had chest pain when you were not performing any physical activity?

Q4. Do you lose your balance because of dizziness, or do you ever lose consciousness?

Q5. Do you have a bone or joint problem (for example, back, knee, or hip) that could be made worse by a change in your physical activity?

Q6. Is your doctor currently prescribing medication for your blood pressure or a heart condition?

Q7. Do you know of any other reason why you should not participate in physical activity?

Q8. Are you currently pregnant or have you given birth in the last 12 months?
