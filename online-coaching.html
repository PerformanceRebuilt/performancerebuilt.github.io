Nutrition support

Practical, evidence-based nutrition guidance built around your training — not generic advice.

Nutrition is a core part of how you perform, recover, and change your body. All coaching plans include some level of nutrition support — tailored to your goals, your schedule, and what's realistic for you to actually follow.

Guidance is built around Australian Dietary Guidelines and delivered as educational frameworks — giving you the knowledge to make better decisions, not just a rigid meal plan to follow blindly.

Sample meal guides
Practical, flexible meal guides built around your calorie and protein targets — designed as educational templates, not strict prescriptions.
Calorie & macro targets
Goal-specific targets calculated for your body, training load, and timeline — whether that's fat loss, muscle building, or performance fuelling.
Nutrition education
Understanding why the numbers are what they are — so you're making informed decisions, not just following a plan you don't understand.
Ongoing adjustments
Nutrition guidance is reviewed and adjusted alongside your training program as your body adapts and your goals evolve.

Important: Nutrition guidance provided through Performance Rebuilt is for educational purposes only, designed for generally healthy individuals, and does not replace advice from a registered dietitian or medical professional. If you have a diagnosed medical condition, eating disorder, or specific clinical dietary requirement, please consult a qualified healthcare professional before commencing any nutrition program.
