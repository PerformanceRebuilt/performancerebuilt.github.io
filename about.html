The story

Not just a coach.
A practitioner.

Most people find their way into fitness coaching through a single passion. For me, it started earlier and went deeper. From my teenage years I was already coaching — helping others train, understanding what worked, and developing an instinct for programming that only comes from years of doing the work yourself.

I competed across AFL, cricket, athletics, boxing, and netball — never at an elite level, but always with serious intent. That experience across multiple sports gave me something textbooks can't: a genuine understanding of how different bodies move, respond to load, and break down under pressure.

"The best coaches aren't just educated — they've lived it. Every program I write is informed by 14 years of being in the trenches."

I went straight from school into formal study — completing a Bachelor of Science in Exercise and Sports Science alongside a Bachelor of Science in Physiotherapy. While others were learning theory, I was already applying it. Over 14 years, the two worlds merged: the science of how the body works, and the art of making it perform.

What sets Performance.Rebuilt apart is that physiotherapy lens. Most coaches program for progress. I program for progress and longevity — understanding injury risk, movement dysfunction, and how to build a body that performs for the long haul, not just the next 12 weeks.

Coaching philosophy

How I believe
training should work

01
Master the basics first

The fundamentals are fundamental for a reason. Before complexity, before intensity, before anything else — the bread and butter movements, done well, done consistently, will take you further than any advanced protocol ever could. I build athletes from the ground up, and I make no apologies for it.

02
Consistency over intensity

The best program is the one you actually follow. Results are not built in single sessions — they're built across months and years of showing up, applying effort, and recovering properly. I design programs that fit your life, because a program you can sustain will always outperform one you can't.

03
Intent in every rep

Going through the motions builds nothing. Every set, every session needs a purpose — and you should know what that purpose is. I coach with full context so you're not just executing a program, you understand why it's built the way it is. That understanding is what separates athletes who progress from those who plateau.

04
Build to last

My physiotherapy background means I think about longevity as much as performance. Getting strong matters. Staying healthy enough to keep getting strong matters more. Every program is designed with injury prevention baked in — not as an afterthought, but as a core principle.

05
Evidence over trend

The fitness industry is full of noise. I cut through it. Every method I use is grounded in sports science and physiology — not whatever is trending on social media this week. You deserve coaching built on what actually works, not what looks good online.

06
The whole athlete

Training doesn't happen in isolation. Sleep, stress, nutrition, life — it all feeds into how you perform and recover. Good coaching accounts for all of it. I work with the full picture of who you are, not just what happens inside the gym.
