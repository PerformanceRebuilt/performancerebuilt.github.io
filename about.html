The story behind the coaching — and why it matters for your results.

Most people find their way into fitness coaching through a single passion. For me, it started earlier and went deeper. From my teenage years I was already coaching — helping others train, understanding what worked, and developing an instinct for programming that only comes from years of doing the work yourself.

Playing many sports growing up gave me something textbooks can't: a genuine understanding of how different bodies move, respond to load, and break down under pressure.

"The best coaches aren't just educated — they've lived it. Every program I write is shaped by 14 years of real coaching, real people, and real results."

I went on to complete a Bachelor of Science in Exercise and Sports Science — but while others were learning theory, I was already applying it. Over 14 years, the science of how the body works merged with the art of making it perform.

What sets Performance Rebuilt apart is that deep science background. Most coaches program for progress. I program for progress and longevity — understanding injury risk, movement mechanics, and how to build a body that performs for the long haul, not just the next 12 weeks.

How I believe
training should work.

Master the basics first

The fundamentals are fundamental for a reason. Before complexity, before intensity — bread and butter movements, done well and done consistently, will take you further than any advanced protocol ever could.

Consistency over novelty

The best program is the one you actually follow. Results are built across months and years of showing up, applying effort, and recovering properly. I design programs that fit your life.

Build to last

Getting strong matters. Staying healthy enough to keep getting strong matters more. Longevity is a core principle of every program — built in from day one, not bolted on as an afterthought.

Evidence over trend

The fitness industry is full of noise. I cut through it. Every method I use is grounded in sports science and evidence — not whatever is trending on social media this week.
